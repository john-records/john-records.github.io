The One Heart Practice

A Practice by John Records with Doug Matchett. Simple movements and perceptions to realize the One Heart.

Namaste

See your own reflection in your partner.

With a partner in front of you, or a mental partner if no one is physically present, slowly bring your palms together. Notice how your consciousness changes.

Mentally acknowledge the Divine, by whatever name is comfortable for you, in your partner. Bow your head a little in humility.

Stage One: My Heart

Stack your palms over your heart. Feel your own physical heart with gratitude. Feelings and emotions may arise: let them.

Wherever you go, wherever you look, you'll never find anyone more worthy of love than you.

Don't rush past this. The love we offer the world includes love of ourself.

Stage Two: Our Hearts

Let your hands open outward, as if welcoming someone in. Open your heart to your partner's, and receive their heart with gratitude and appreciation.

Linger, fall in love with anyone with whom you practice.

Stage Three: More Hearts

Open your hands and arms wider. Bring to heart the people given to you to love — family, friends, the people at work, the stranger at the market. Feel the connections that are already there.

You might feel awe, the cup of your heart might run over with love.

Stage Four: Open to All Hearts

Open your arms wide. Now include everyone: the suffering and the joyful, the people you find difficult, even those who cause harm, living and deceased.

Your broken-open heart has room for the whole of Creation.

Stage Five: Gathering All Hearts

While lovingly sensing All Hearts, the Gatherer of Hearts — called by different names in different traditions — gently moves the arms in a bringing-together motion, and gathers all hearts into One Heart, where they have always been.

This culminates in the praying hands, the Namaste gesture, which is not other than One Heart. Rest here a moment.

It is one heart, beating in all of us, reflected in all of us, and you are an aspect of it.

Stage Six: The Return

After practice get up, and let what you touched move your hands and feet. Putting your hand to your heart during the day can help you to remember who you are.

Yours are the hands of love, yours are the feet of love.

Three Cycles

Do the full cycle three times, or as many times as you wish, at whatever pace. Two or three breaths in each position is enough.

You might also find that even one cycle of the practice suffices to shift your awareness to that of the One Heart.

I. The First Cycle

This is not a rote practice. It may vary from day to day: sometimes grief, sometimes love, sometimes joy, stillness, peace.

Let come whatever may come.

II. The Second Cycle

You might notice that the person who began the practice has changed, opened, during the practice.

Pause and linger and savor at any place.

III. The Third Cycle

Let One Heart, the One Person, engage in the practice, to know itself more fully, to waken more fully in, through and as you.

Thus Love incarnates.

The One Person

It works and plays with the insight that we each and all are aspects of the One Person. Christians call that Jesus. Not other than Krishna and the rest of the pantheon of avatars.

Our hearts are an aspect of, a facet of, a gateway to, One Heart.

Drawing from Hindu, Buddhist, Christian and Jewish traditions. The One Heart is depicted as the Sacred Heart of Jesus in Catholicism. Call it by whatever name is comfortable for you.

Please experiment

The gestures are optional and not prescriptive. You don't have to do them this way. All Hearts can be intimidating, so be prudent in your opening to suffering, and open if you feel it's right for you.

Let it feed you.
