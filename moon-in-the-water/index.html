In the Shelter of Each Other

This video was made for an online zoom community to support them. In light of the unstable and challenging world, how do we make our way? We can remember that we live in the shelter of each other, and that we don't have to do this alone. Guided Meditation: Kuan Yin Mantra

April 1, 201713 min

"Namo Kuan Shih Yin Pusa" is the mantra (or mantram) we use to invite the support of the bodhisattva Kuan Yin, who hears the cries of the world. In this video, the Kuan Yin mantra is recited 108 times, and the use of the mantra is explained. Books referenced in the video are Becoming Kuan Yin by Stephen Levine, and The Mantram Handbook by Eknath Easwaran.

Listening Guided Meditation

February 24, 20176 min

This simple guided meditation uses a chime for focus. You can listen to the chime, and listen to the silence after the chime fades. A gong or bell also can be used. A Woodstock Zenergy Chime is used in this video.
